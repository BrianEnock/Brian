How It Works

Learn how to participate and earn with BLSOLS.COM

Complete Process Guide

1. Registration & Account Creation
To register and create an account with Blsols.com is free. Your M-Pesa number becomes your account number. Valid National ID is required for verification.

2. Package Selection & Fees
Blsols.com charges 20% for 5/10 days packages and 50% for the 24HRS package. Minimum amount is ksh1000/- and maximum amount is ksh20,000/-

3. M-Pesa Paybill Payment
After selecting your package, you'll receive an M-Pesa STK Push notification on your phone. Use Paybill Number: 522533 and Account Number: 8059564

4. Claiming Donations
After expiry of the Maturity Period, you can claim your donation. A recommitment fee of 20% (50% for 24HRS package) will be deducted automatically to keep the system sustainable.

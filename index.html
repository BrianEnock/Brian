How It Works

Learn how participation works on BLSOLS.COM.

Complete Process Guide

1. Registration & Account Creation
Registration is free and takes effect immediately — no email confirmation step. Your M-Pesa number becomes your account number. A valid National ID is required, and accounts are checked by the back office.

2. Package Selection & Fees
BLSOLS charges 20% for the 5/10-day packages and 50% for the 24HRS package. Minimum amount is Ksh 1,000 and maximum is Ksh 20,000.

3. M-Pesa Paybill Payment
After selecting your package, pay via Paybill Number 522533, Account Number 8059564. A payment confirmation is then matched by back-office staff against the M-Pesa statement.

4. Claiming
Once the maturity period expires, you may claim. A recommitment fee of 20% (50% for the 24HRS package) is deducted to keep the pool funded.
